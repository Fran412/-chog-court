# Demo video script (about 2:30, limit is 3:00)

The goal is to prove two things in order: it is fun, and the Chog cannot be removed. Record in a 1300px-wide browser window.

**0:00 – 0:15 · Hook.** On the empty docket, say: "The internet argues about everything. Chog Court gives those arguments a judge, and the judge is your Chog."

**0:15 – 0:50 · A ruling.** Pick Chog #0066 (Stern, a Judge) from the row under the nameplate. Open "The Floor Price Heist". Read the charge aloud, point out the bench-only exhibit ("only a Judge sees this one"), then rule Not guilty and let the stamp land. Pause on the outcome and the record line.

**0:50 – 1:15 · The reputation.** Point at the brass nameplate across the top. Say: "This Chog now has an accuracy and a title. It is already a Chief Justice, and every ruling moves it." Switch to Chog #2750, the Court Clown, and say: "This one keeps getting it wrong, and the whole court can see it."

**1:15 – 1:50 · The part that proves the NFT matters.** Back on Chog #0417 (92% over 37 cases). Click Simulate a sale. Say: "The wallet changed. The record did not. It is stored against the token, not the wallet, so a Chief Justice Chog is worth more than a blank one." Remove the Chog in your head: with no Chog there is no role, no exhibits, no rank and no history.

**1:50 – 2:15 · Retention.** Show the daily docket (three new cases), the streak on the nameplate, and the Roll of the bench. Say: "Every day there are new cases, and every ruling changes your Chog's standing."

**2:15 – 2:30 · What's next.** "Live jury votes on real community disputes, appeals, and rank titles that holders can show off." End on the leaderboard.

## Testing instructions for the submission form

1. Open the demo link. No wallet is needed: six sample Chogs are built in.
2. Pick any case on the left, read the exhibits, and rule Guilty or Not guilty.
3. Use the row of Chogs under the nameplate to switch Chogs, and press Simulate a sale to see the record stay with the token. The Roll of the bench shows the leaderboard.
4. With a wallet holding a Chog, Connect wallet loads your real tokens and records rulings to the shared court record.

## Before you record

- Do a dry run so the cases you pick are still unruled. Ruled cases show no vote buttons. To reset, clear the site's localStorage.
- If the shared backend is on, the leaderboard shows real tokens only, so it will look sparse until people have ruled. For the video, record in demo mode.
